Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

ScreenStacka is built to respect your privacy. Here's exactly what we collect, what we don't, and why.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. Plausible does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and does not track you across websites. All data is aggregated — we see page view counts and referral sources, never individual visitors. Plausible is open source, hosted in the EU, and fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR without requiring a cookie banner.

Cookies

We don't use any. ScreenStacka sets zero cookies. There's no cookie banner because there are no cookies.

Personal Information

We don't collect names, email addresses, or any personal information through the site. If you email us directly, we'll have your email address from that exchange — we won't share it or add it to any list.

Affiliate Links

ScreenStacka contains affiliate links to Amazon.com. When you click these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission. We have no access to any data about your activity on Amazon.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service loaded on this site is Plausible Analytics, described above.

Data Storage

Your comparison settings (the TV sizes you enter) are stored in the URL hash fragment. This data stays in your browser and is never sent to any server. If you use the Share button, you're sharing a URL that contains those settings — nothing more.

Children's Privacy

ScreenStacka does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The site doesn't collect information from anyone, period.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Given how little data we collect, meaningful changes are unlikely.

Questions about this policy? Get in touch.
